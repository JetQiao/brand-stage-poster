# 白字红光示例（默认风格）

对应图片：[assets/preview.png](../assets/preview.png)。预设 `white-red`；旧版 [暗红立体字](preview-dark-red-prompt.md) 仍可选。

生成方式：内置 image_gen 文生图；未使用输入图片、真人照片或第三方 Logo。文字 `YOUR BRAND` 为通用占位文案，顶部 `BRAND STAGE` 是本次明确指定的示例标签，不是所有海报的必加文案。生成输出为 1870 × 841 PNG，约 2.22:1，未缩放。本例没有提供图形 Logo，因此保留中央留白；有用户提供的 Logo 时可采用高置的中央标志。

白色字面、红色背光和台阶灯带、深红黑背景与红白镜面反射是本预设的视觉基准。该示例不证明人像保真或所有自由度档位均已验证。

Their front faces are luminous warm white, large flat clean bright white surfaces. Strong scarlet red backlighting and red edge bloom surrounds their contours, with subtle red extrusion sides. White faces are the dominant bright visual, not dark red or red metallic letter fronts. Letters run from approximately 17% to 66% down the frame, wide panoramic impact with modest margins. Dark burgundy-to-black smooth gradient backdrop with strong red glow behind the letters. No actual graphic logo is provided: keep the central upper gap elegant and empty of symbols. Do not invent a monogram, diamond, geometric emblem or placeholder logo.
Wide shallow tiered stage with two restrained horizontal red LED edge lines. Foreground is a very smooth near-black mirror floor, low roughness, clear bright WHITE letter reflections together with vivid RED light reflections. No visible tile pattern, no concrete texture. A trace of atmospheric haze behind the stage.
Symmetrical frontal composition, clean monumental visual hierarchy, graphic precision, premium brand-launch photography, sharply readable typography, controlled bloom retaining clear letter boundaries, red-white-black color family. No people, no machines, furniture, decorative props, walls with columns, corner microcopy, watermark, border or collage.
